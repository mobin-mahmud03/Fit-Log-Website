# FITLOG

FitLog is a clean and easy-to-use fitness app. It helps you find great exercises, make your daily workout plans, save your favorite moves, and track your workout progress without any hassle.

---

## Built With

- Next.js: For fast web page loading and smooth navigation.
- Tailwind CSS: To make the app look modern and clean on all screens.
- Lucide React: For clean and simple icons.
- React Context API: To keep track of your saved workouts and plans smoothly.
- TypeScript: To write clean and safe code.

---

## 5 Main Features

1. Browse All Workouts: Easily look through a complete list of exercises to target different muscle groups.
2. Make Your Plan & Save Favorites: Add workouts to your daily plan or save them for later with just one click.
3. Live Stats Tracking: Watch your total exercise count, workout minutes, and burned calories update automatically.
4. Sort Your Way: Quickly sort your workouts by duration, calories, or ratings.
5. Mark as Done: Tap the check button when you finish a workout to cross it off your list with a neat little notification.
